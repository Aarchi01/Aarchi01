## Hi, I'm Aarchi Gandhi

Growing up, my first "data visualisation" was a hand-drawn dog wearing a customised neck belt, eating chocolate — stuck proudly in the front window of my father's veterinary pharmacy. I was convinced it drove sales. (The product probably helped too, but let me have that one.) Helping with his business as I grew up, I was already tracking what drove sales, which products moved, who the customers were; I just didn't have a name for it yet. That curiosity followed me all the way to an MSc in Data Science at the University of Roehampton, London. 
Now I get to build the kind of systems and reports I wish we'd had back then turning complex data into something anyone can understand and act on. I'm naturally curious and quietly observant, the kind of person who gets genuinely excited when the data starts telling a story.

🔍 **Open to data analyst and data scientist roles in UK retail and supply chain.**

## 📌 Featured Projects

### 🌱 [Strawberry Greenhouse EDA](https://github.com/Aarchi01/Strawberry-Greenhouse-EDA)
Environmental analysis of greenhouse sensor data focused on VPD optimisation, temperature differential patterns, and early detection of powdery mildew risk conditions. Demonstrates domain-applied analytical thinking on real agricultural IoT data.
`Python` `Pandas` `Seaborn` `EDA` `Data Cleaning`

### 🗣 [Text-to-SQL Natural Language Interface](https://github.com/Aarchi01/Text-to-SQL-LI)
LLM-powered system that translates plain English questions into executable SQL queries and delivers results back in human language. Built during an industry internship; includes a custom evaluation framework measuring execution accuracy and SQL validity.
`Python` `LLMs` `SQL` `NLP`

### 📊 [Sales Performance Analysis](https://github.com/Aarchi01/Sales-Performance-Analysis)
End-to-end exploratory analysis on sales data, identifying key revenue drivers and customer segments. Includes interactive visualisations and summary insights.
`Python` `Pandas` `Seaborn` `Jupyter`

### 📝 [Text Summarisation with Bi-LSTM & BART](https://github.com/Aarchi01/Text-Summarization-Using-Bi-LSTM-and-BART)
Deep learning pipeline comparing Bi-LSTM and BART transformer architectures for abstractive text summarisation, with ROUGE-score evaluation.
`HuggingFace` `NLP` `TensorFlow`
